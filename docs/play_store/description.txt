SHORT DESCRIPTION (66/80 characters)
------------------------------------------------
Private AI that runs entirely on your phone. No cloud, no account.

FULL DESCRIPTION (1540/4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------
InstaLLM runs language models on your device. Your prompts, chats and files stay
in the app's private storage; nothing is sent to a server to generate a reply,
and the app keeps working with the network off once a model is installed.

BRING YOUR OWN MODEL
Import a GGUF model from storage or download one you pick from Hugging Face.
The model list shows size, quantisation and whether a vision projector is
available, so you can match a model to your phone's memory.

CHAT THAT STAYS LOCAL
Streaming replies, stop at any time, multiple chats with search, pin and
rename, per-model memory and personas. Switching models keeps the conversation
history and records which model wrote each reply.

VOICE, VISION AND READING
Dictate with your microphone, have replies read aloud, and attach a photo to a
vision model. Speech is transcribed on the device; images are downscaled and
their metadata stripped before they reach the model.

BENCHMARKS YOU CONTROL
Run the same prompt across your installed models and compare measured speed on
your own hardware. Results stay on the phone.

WORKSPACES, BOTS AND ROOMS
Organise chats into workspaces, create bots with their own instructions, and
use rooms for group conversations between bots. Everything is stored locally.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry. The only network
requests are a model download you start and an API endpoint you configure
yourself. Delete chats, models and attachments from inside the app.
