# InstaLLM privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

InstaLLM runs AI models on your device. It has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no server that receives your conversations.

## What is processed on your device

- **Prompts, conversations, personas, bots, rooms and notes.** Written to the
  app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere by the
  app.
- **Microphone audio.** Used only when you record a voice note or use the voice
  companion. The clip is transcribed by a local model and is not sent off the
  device. The app asks for the microphone permission at the moment you use it.
- **Images you attach.** Processed by a local vision model on the device. You can
  turn on downscaling and metadata removal before attachment.
- **Model files.** Downloaded only when you ask for one, from the Hugging Face
  URL you choose, or imported from storage.

## Network use

The app works offline once models are installed. It uses the network only for
actions you start:

- downloading a model you selected from Hugging Face or another catalog;
- a model endpoint or API provider you configured yourself in Settings.

Those requests carry the model file request or the message you sent to that
provider; InstaLLM does not send usage statistics, identifiers, device details
or benchmark results.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** for voice notes and the voice companion.
- **Notifications** for generation and download progress.
- **Internet** for the optional downloads above.

## Data you control

Chats, models, attachments and settings live in the app's storage and can be
deleted from inside the app (delete a chat, remove a model, clear the workspace
or uninstall). Backups you export are written where you choose and contain no
model weights, credentials or attachment binaries.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This policy is updated in the repository at
`docs/play_store/privacy_policy.md` whenever the app's data behaviour changes.
